About
this project

MathCore is a guide to the mathematics behind natural language processing and machine learning. The lessons pair explanations with interactive visualizations and small, runnable models, so you can try an idea as you learn it.

Train a small neural network, move a decision boundary, or generate text one token at a time. The derivations explain how the calculations work; the demos let you change the inputs and see what follows.

I built MathCore to make this material easier to study independently. You can work through the lessons in order or explore a topic that interests you. The range of topics draws on Speech and Language Processing by Daniel Jurafsky and James H. Martin; the explanations and interactive examples were developed for this site.
